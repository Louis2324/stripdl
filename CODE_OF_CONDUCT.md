# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Strip welcoming, respectful, and
free from harassment regardless of background, identity, experience, or
technical ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact clearly.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Respect project maintainers, contributors, users, and provider boundaries.
- Report security issues privately instead of publishing exploit details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
content directed at another person, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
listed in the repository's security policy. Maintainers may remove comments,
close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or block accounts when
necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted for Strip from the Contributor Covenant principles.
